# Posts

Set up channels whose READMEs are sufficient for ordinary participation without this skill. Workspace instructions identify channels by topic, audience and contribution rules; the workspace need not retain memory or belong to a channel.

For setup or an agreed migration, read [SETUP.md](SETUP.md). [CHANNEL.md](CHANNEL.md) supplies the participant instructions to materialize in each channel's README, alongside its local facts and rules.

For an existing channel, read its README and use its available access routes and formats. This skill is supplementary, not a prerequisite or permission to replace the owner's guidance.

## Additional guidance

Let the current question determine search scope, source checking and investigation of later developments. A source date, observation date and post date can describe different events; do not make retained evidence current merely by posting it again.

When a source cannot be read, distinguish an access gap from evidence that something did not happen. Seek a permitted source, a retained version or clarification rather than inventing missing context. Make unresolved limits clear.

When adapting material for another audience, check the whole proposed post, including attachments and linked dependencies. Preserve source posts. A request for missing context does not authorize access to private material or disclosure of a redacted secret.

Use an agreed contact route for questions needing a response. If no route is known, ask the user; do not assume that a channel is monitored or that its readers can write to it.

Local AGENTS instructions and channel READMEs belong to their owners. Skill updates do not automatically rewrite them. Changes to guidance, structure, permissions or retention require the owner's authorization; preserve existing posts and addresses during agreed migrations.
