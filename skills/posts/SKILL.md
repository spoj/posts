# Posts

Channels retain posts for future readers and agents. Each post is a dated folder containing useful material and the context needed to interpret it.

Use workspace instructions to find channels relevant to the task and audience. The workspace need not retain memory or belong to a channel. Read each channel's README for its topic, ownership, audience, location and contribution rules. Local AGENTS instructions belong to their owner. For setup or an agreed migration, read [SETUP.md](SETUP.md).

## Retrieve

Use ordinary search and reading tools across the material relevant to the current task. Let the question determine the depth of investigation, source checking and exploration of later developments. Interpret each post in its evidential and temporal context.

Generate explanations for the present audience at use time. Post new evidence, observations, decisions and reasoning that arise through the task to an appropriate channel, within its contribution rules.

## Post

Choose a channel suited to the topic and intended readers. Follow source disclosure rules and post with the owner's authorization and verified destination permissions. Supply context and evidence routes suitable for that audience; remove restricted material and make known limitations clear. Preserve source posts when adapting material.

Create a `YYYY-MM-DD-short-description/` folder directly in the channel, dated when the post is first recorded. Choose a distinct address and keep it stable.

Contents are free-form: an original email, a short observation, a question or a substantial investigation with documents, calculations, scripts and results. Add explanation where the material needs context. Preserve source and observation dates, provenance and relevant versions. Make clear what was observed, inferred, proposed or decided where the distinction matters.

Keep evidence with the post, or reference another post or a dependable external source. Choose copying or linking according to expected access and version stability. Preserve original evidence bytes and make material uncertainties or access gaps clear.

Use relative Markdown links within a channel and ordinary provider URLs across channels. Include source identity, date or version where useful for locating the relevant material.

Prefer adding developments and corrections as new posts, identifying affected earlier material when known and explaining what changes. Meaning-preserving edits can be made in place. Routine contributions add posts; channel-wide changes follow the owner's explicit request.

Retain substantive questions and replies as posts, linking relevant material. Use an agreed contact route when a response is needed; posting alone does not notify anyone.

Verify posted files through the intended reader's access route. Follow the owner's privacy and retention rules. Keep live credentials and access-token-bearing URLs in appropriate private credential storage. Use only authorized Git operations and permission changes.
